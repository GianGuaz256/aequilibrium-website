            Methodology

            How to read these numbers.

            Currency
            Strategy NAV and all positions are in US dollars. The portfolio starts at $1,000,000 on January 1, 2025 and ends at $1,122,801.79 on December 31, 2025.
            Benchmark
            XEON is the Xtrackers II EUR Overnight Rate Swap UCITS ETF 1C (ISIN LU0290358497), on Xetra prices in euros with no currency conversion. Days without trading carry the last close. Its daily returns are levered 2.78× so that its volatility equals the strategy’s.
            Risk-free rate
            2.25% a year, an approximation of the average €STR over the period, converted to a daily rate for Sharpe, Sortino, Calmar and Martin.
            Annualisation
            Return = (final NAV / initial NAV)^(365/364) − 1. Volatility = sample standard deviation of daily returns × √365, since the strategy runs 365 days a year.
            Ratios
            Sortino uses the root mean square of negative excess returns only. Calmar = annual excess return / |maximum drawdown|. The Ulcer Index is the root mean square of percentage drawdowns over all 365 NAVs; Martin = annual excess return × 100 / Ulcer Index.
            Simulation limits
            Pendle PTs are valued with implied rather than effective fixed APY, and the 5% slippage exclusion rule for stablecoins could not be applied. No transaction costs, slippage or fees are deducted.
            Leverage scenarios
            Levered XEON figures assume daily compounding and no financing cost. A real leveraged position would pay for financing and earn less.

            Important information

            The figures on this page come from a backtest simulation, not from actual trading. Simulated and past performance are not reliable indicators of future results. This page is for information only and is not an offer, a solicitation or investment advice.
